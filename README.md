This is a visual novel script that tells a philosophical dialogue between two characters, Arthur and Elena, exploring themes of existentialism and the meaning of life.

Core Story: Arthur is in a state of despair, feeling trapped in meaningless routine (the "Sisyphus rock" metaphor). Elena tries to pull him out by offering different philosophical perspectives. The game presents two main dialogue paths:

    Path A - Logic & Rebellion: Elena argues for philosophical rebellion against nihilism using Camus and absurdism. The path branches into:
        A bad ending where Arthur rejects hope as false comfort
        A good ending where he accepts living "out of spite" against the void—finding meaning in his own existence

    Path B - Senses & Connection: Elena shifts the focus from abstract logic to lived experience and human connection. This branches into:
        A neutral/bad ending where hedonism fails to motivate him
        A good ending where her physical presence and promise of "breakfast together" anchors him to the present moment

Key Themes:

    Nihilism vs. Absurdism (with Wikipedia links for context)
    The Sisyphus metaphor (pushing a rock up a hill forever)
    Finding meaning through spite, sensory experience, and human connection
    Choice and agency in the face of cosmic indifference

The script uses visual scenes, music cues, and branching dialogue to create an interactive exploration of existential philosophy through intimate character interaction.
